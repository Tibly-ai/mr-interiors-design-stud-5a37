SAMANTHA ABRAMS

Samantha Abrams is the founder of MR. INTERIORS DESIGN STUDIO, a New York based interior design atelier created to serve men who want a considered, well executed space without managing the process themselves.

Raised by an interior designer, Samantha developed an early understanding of how space functions and how layout, proportion, material, and light shape daily life. Her work is guided by structure and clarity rather than trend, with each project approached as a complete system rather than a collection of individual pieces.

Raised by an interior designer, Samantha developed an early understanding of how space functions and how layout, proportion, material, and light shape daily life. Her work is guided by structure and clarity rather than trend, with each project approached as a complete system rather than a collection of individual pieces.

The studio’s design language is clean, restrained, and architectural, often informed by mid-century modern principles. Emphasis is placed on intelligent planning, curated selections, and decisive editing so clients are presented with a refined set of options rather than endless choices. The goal is to reduce friction while improving the final result.

Samantha studied real estate at NYU-Schack Institute of Real Estate, providing a technical foundation in how spaces are developed and used. She brings operational experience from residential and commercial environments, where precision, coordination, and integrity were essential. She is also a licensed real estate professional.

Her professional background includes contributing to projects at Tali Oren Design, gaining experience in translating concept into execution, as well as time at Stark Carpet, deepening her understanding of material performance and finish. Most recently, she supported the development of a design installation at Salon Art + Design in New York working alongside international designers and galleries.

Outside of the studio, Samantha draws inspiration from travel and personal narratives, observing how people live and move through different cultures and settings.

MR. INTERIORS DESIGN STUDIO was founded to offer a more efficient and intentional alternative for clients who value good design but do not want to manage the process themselves.
